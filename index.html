Section Three

AI Prompts

These are the two prompts used to make the quiz and the feedback.

Prompt 1

Quiz Generation

“Act as a mathematics teacher. Create a digital quiz with 7 multiple-choice word problems for school students. Include four answer options for each question, one correct answer, and a complete answer key. Use clear and age-appropriate language.”
Prompt 2

Feedback Generation

“Act as an experienced mathematics teacher. Evaluate five different student answers to the same word problem. Identify mathematical mistakes, explain what each student did correctly or incorrectly, and provide supportive, specific feedback. Do not only give the correct answer; help students understand their mistakes.”

Section Five

My Reflection on Using AI for Assessment

AI helped me create mathematics quiz questions quickly and generate different types of student feedback. It saved time and provided examples of strong, average, and weak student answers. I also learned that AI can help teachers identify common mathematical mistakes. However, AI is not always accurate. Sometimes its feedback can be too general or may not explain a mistake clearly enough. Therefore, I checked every calculation and improved the feedback to make it more useful for students. I believe AI should support teachers, not replace them. Teachers must review AI-generated content before using it in the classroom. Next time, I would create more interactive activities and test the platform with real students.
